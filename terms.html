Who can use Hap

You must be 18 or older, or have the consent of a parent or guardian. Hap is offered in South Africa.

Hap is not medical advice

Hap is a food diary. It isn't a medical device, and it doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Targets and estimates come from general formulas for adults, not from an assessment of you.

Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing how you eat, especially if you're pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition such as diabetes or kidney disease, take medication, or have or have had an eating disorder.

If tracking food starts to feel harmful, stop and talk to someone. SADAG's Mental Health Helpline is free and open 24 hours: 0800 456 789. In an emergency, call 112 from a mobile phone.

Food data can be wrong

Nutrition values come from USDA FoodData Central, from Open Food Facts, which anyone can edit, and from what people type, including foods other Hap users share. They can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and a barcode match is no guarantee of the product in your hand. We check that shared foods make sense, not that they match the product, and we don't guarantee any values.

What Claude suggests from a meal or recipe photo is an estimate. It can miss foods, mistake one for another, misread a recipe and misjudge amounts. Check it before you log or save it.

Hap doesn't track allergens or ingredients. If you have an allergy or intolerance, always read the product's own label.
