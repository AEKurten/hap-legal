Deleting your Hap account

How to delete your account in Hap: Food Diary, the app by Aubrey Kurten (ChenexaSoftware on Google Play), and the information backed up with it.

Updated 12 November 2026

In the app

Open Hap, go to Settings, Account, and tap Delete account. You can keep your diary on the phone or remove it too. Your account and its backup are removed from Hap's server straight away.

Without the app

Email hapapp.za@gmail.com from the address you sign in to Hap with, asking for your account to be deleted. Hap deletes the account and its backup within 30 days and replies to confirm. Hap may ask you to confirm that the request comes from your account.

What is deleted

Your account (your email address and account ID) and everything backed up with it: your diary, the foods you created, saved meals, recipes, targets and weight.

Your account ID is removed from foods you shared and reports you made. The shared foods themselves stay, because other people may rely on them, but nothing links them to you.

Copies in the hosting provider's routine backups are deleted automatically as those backups expire, within 30 days. The server's technical logs, such as sign-in times, are deleted within a few days.

Nothing else to delete

Without an account, everything stays on your phone, and deleting the app deletes it. What Hap reads from Apple Health or Health Connect is never stored by Hap.
